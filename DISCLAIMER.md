# Disclaimer

The Healthcare Firewall Migration Toolkit (the planner and templates) supports good practice when planning a firewall migration. It is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

- It is **not legal advice**. It does not tell you what any law requires of your organization.
- It is **not a compliance certification**. Completing it does not make an organization compliant with HIPAA or any other rule.
- The **readiness check** is a planning aid. It does not replace the judgement of the people responsible for the change.
- Thresholds, roles and steps are starting points. Adapt them to your own systems and risk tolerance.
- **Do not enter protected health information** (patient names, record numbers or similar) into the planner or templates.

Where the toolkit refers to the HIPAA Security Rule, HHS Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, check the current official versions of those documents.
